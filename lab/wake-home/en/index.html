Process

From spec to delivery
01 / 04

Every step has a clear output. Scope is agreed before work starts.

  1. 01 · Alignment

    Pin down the scope first

    You share specs, CAD or photos, where it will be used (website, trade show, e-commerce) and your deadline. I come back with an approach and a quote.

    OutputScope sheet: deliverables, revision rounds, timeline

  2. 02 · Storyboard & look

    Agree on direction before render time

    Shot list, materials and lighting direction, proven with one look-frame or an animatic, so nobody discovers the wrong direction at the end.

    OutputOne look-frame or animatic

  3. 03 · Production

    Renders, animation and web in step

    Built to the agreed direction. Each stage includes a fixed number of revision rounds; new requests outside scope are quoted separately, so the project keeps moving.

    OutputStage deliverables and revision log

  4. 04 · Delivery

    Formats you can actually use

    Delivered for the job: 4K stills, MP4 / WebM video, GLB models for the web or the full page code, with usage notes.

    OutputFinal files and delivery checklist

FAQ

Before we work together
How is pricing set?

By scope: number of stills, seconds of animation, shots, and whether web integration is needed. Fill in the form below or email your specs; a formal quote follows once the brief is clear.

How many revisions are included?

Each stage includes a fixed number of rounds, written into the scope sheet. New requests outside scope are quoted separately so the project can close on time.

What do I need to provide?

CAD or 3D files are ideal (STEP, OBJ, FBX all work). A physical product or photos also work, with extra modelling time. Add brand colours, where it will be used and any references.

How long does it take?

It varies a lot — a single still and a full product film are different orders of magnitude. You get a concrete timeline once the brief is confirmed; mention rush deadlines up front.

Can I hire just 3D, or just web?

Yes. Each service line can be commissioned on its own. When you need both, one person handles assets and page together, so they fit exactly.
