Zhonghe Policy Map

DATA VISUALIZATION · CHINESE ONLY

For the 2026 New Taipei council race in Zhonghe, this map pins one candidate's local pledges and past record onto the district's 93 villages: tap a marker and the list on the right lights up, and the other way round. A second 3D "quest map" turns pledges into beacons and district-wide pledges into a quest log. Every item is sourced from public news reports; where reports give no exact site, the area is drawn as a dashed region instead of a fake precise pin.

WAKE-015 / 2026

LIVE

For Your Own Good

NARRATIVE GAME · CHINESE ONLY

A game about the controlling Asian parent. Eight levels, eight ages from 7 to 30: you plan your week after school, Mum crosses out the slots she doesn't like in red pen, and you decide whether to obey, talk it through, or nod and do it anyway. The tighter her control, the wider the door stays open — then come the camera, the phone password and location sharing. Exams at 15, university choices at 18 and Lunar New Year dinner at 30 are the boss levels. The soundtrack is a music-box Für Elise, with a metronome that gets louder as her control grows.

WAKE-014 / 2026

LIVE

Eight Years, One Festival

CIVIC SATIRE · CHINESE ONLY

A satirical city-hall simulator set in Taichung. You take office at the end of 2018 with eight years of budgets: spend on a shopping festival that makes tomorrow's news, or on an incinerator, a metro line and food-waste readiness that take years to show? Eight years are eight levels: mechanics unlock year by year, each level has a goal and a star, and the 2022 election and the 2025 swine-fever outbreak are boss levels. The ending sets your city beside the real headlines, with sources and the city government's responses. The music has two layers — the more you pour into the festival, the louder it gets.

Luku

POINT-AND-CLICK PUZZLE

A 2D point-and-click puzzle game set during Taiwan's Luku Incident of 1952. A granddaughter in the present and the villagers of that winter take turns across four chapters: hiding a copybook, thumbprinting a record he cannot read, being made to shout on a mountain road, scratching eighty-three days into a wall. Events follow the Control Yuan's findings and the National Human Rights Museum's public records; the characters are fictional.
