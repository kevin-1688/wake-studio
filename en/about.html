About WAKE

WAKE means to awaken.

I'm Kevin Chang, and WAKE is my one-person studio. Taiwan's history often feels far away when you read about it. A single line in a textbook stands for days that many people actually lived through. I make games so players can step inside and make the choices themselves, instead of watching from the side.

My main project, BÔ BÂNG, is still in development. In the meantime I've made a few shorter games you can play in a browser. Luku is about what happened in the hills of Shiding in 1952. Eight Years, One Festival pokes fun at eight years of Taichung city politics. For Your Own Good is about the kind of parents a lot of us grew up with. There are also three small games about disinformation, money and the defense budget.

Since it's just me, I do the research, modeling, code, art, sound and website myself. It's slow, but I know the reasons behind every decision.
