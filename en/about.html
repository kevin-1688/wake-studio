About WAKE

WAKE means to awaken.

This studio believes Taiwan's history and culture should not just be material to read — they should be things you can experience. That is why I make games: mechanics carrying memory, so the player is not a spectator on the outside but someone living inside it.

Beyond the flagship BÔ BÂNG, WAKE keeps building lightweight web games on public issues — media literacy, financial literacy, defense resilience. Three subjects, one method: putting the player in the decision-maker's seat.

A one-person studio means the entire pipeline runs through the same hands: research, modeling, code, web, marketing assets. Slow — but every link carries the same intent.
